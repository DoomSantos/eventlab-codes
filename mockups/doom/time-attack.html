How To Use Live Timing Leaderboards

How it will work

  1. Load the Time Attack events Dedicated share codes for every track — built for hot laps, not casual cruising. You’ll find them here when we go live.
  2. Drive with the DoomSantos app Connect once, run your session, and your times push up automatically as you cross the line.
  3. Time Attack only Regular EventLab codes stay for fun. Only official Time Attack sessions feed the board — so every result means something.
  4. Chase the board Live Timing shows who’s on track and who’s fastest today. Leaderboards keep the lasting rivals — by track, class, and season.
